# TradeVerified — Continuous Improvement & Product Roadmap

How TradeVerified keeps getting more useful for **tradespeople** and **employers** over time. This is a living document: review it every quarter, reprioritize based on data and feedback, and ship in small, measurable increments.

---

## Guiding principles

1. **Trust is the product.** Every feature should make verification deeper, faster, or more believable.
2. **Both sides must win.** A change that only helps one side weakens the marketplace.
3. **Ship small, measure, iterate.** Prefer many small releases over big-bang launches.
4. **Let data and users lead.** Decisions backed by usage metrics and direct feedback, not guesses.
5. **Reduce friction relentlessly.** Every extra step in onboarding or hiring costs conversions.

---

## How we gather signal (the feedback engine)

- **In-app feedback widget** on every dashboard ("Suggest a feature / report a problem").
- **Onboarding & churn surveys** — why people sign up, why they leave.
- **Analytics**: funnels for signup → profile complete → verified → first application/hire.
- **Support tickets & reviews** — tag and theme recurring issues.
- **Quarterly user interviews** — 5–10 tradespeople and 5–10 employers.
- **A/B testing** for pricing, copy, and onboarding flows.
- **Public roadmap & changelog** so users see their feedback turn into shipped features.

## Roadmap by horizon

### Now / Near-term (0–3 months) — polish & trust
**For tradespeople**
- Profile completeness meter with guided next steps.
- Faster verification flow + status notifications (email + in-app).
- Mobile-optimized profile and uploads (most trades are phone-first).
- Shareable public profile link + QR code for business cards/trucks.

**For employers**
- Saved searches and email alerts for new matching candidates.
- Better application management (stages: new → reviewing → contacted → hired).
- Company verification badge to build candidate trust.

**Platform**
- Performance/SEO pass on public pages.
- Email deliverability + notification preferences.
- Analytics instrumentation for all key funnels.

### Next (3–6 months) — engagement & matching
**For tradespeople**
- In-app messaging with employers (no more email-only).
- Job match recommendations based on trade, location, certs.
- Earnings/rate insights ("plumbers in your area charge X").
- Continuing-education and certification reminders (renewals before expiry).

**For employers**
- Smart candidate ranking and match scores.
- Team seats / multiple recruiters per company.
- Bulk job posting and templates.
- Interview scheduling.

**Platform**
- Reviews v2: response from tradespeople, richer proof-of-service.
- Trust & safety: fraud detection on fake reviews/credentials.
- Native mobile app (or PWA) groundwork.

### Later (6–12 months) — scale & ecosystem
- **Payments & invoicing** with optional escrow.
- **Lead generation** for end customers (homeowners requesting quotes).
- **Enterprise / ATS integrations** (Workday, Greenhouse, etc.).
- **Certification & insurance partnerships** integrated into onboarding.
- **Public API** for partners and large employers.
- **Labor-market insights** dashboard (aggregate, anonymized data).
- **Geographic & trade expansion** playbook, city-by-city.

### Vision (12 months+)
- The default trust layer for the skilled trades — verification that follows a worker across their whole career.
- Multi-region, multi-language support.
- AI-assisted matching, profile building, and job descriptions.

## North-star & supporting metrics

- **North star:** *verified hires made through the platform* (the moment both sides get value).
- **Tradesperson health:** profile completion rate, verification rate, applications sent, profile views.
- **Employer health:** time-to-first-shortlist, applications per job, hires per employer.
- **Marketplace liquidity:** % of jobs that receive qualified applicants; % of searches that lead to contact.
- **Business:** MRR/ARR, free→paid conversion, churn, LTV:CAC.

---

## Prioritization framework (RICE-lite)

For each idea, score **Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort**, and favor work that:
1. Deepens trust/verification,
2. Improves marketplace liquidity (helps both sides connect), and
3. Moves a funnel metric we're actively trying to improve.

> Always ask: *does this help a tradesperson get hired, or an employer hire with more confidence?* If not, it can wait.
