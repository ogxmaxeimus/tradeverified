# TradeVerified — Monetization Strategy

How TradeVerified makes money, who pays, and how revenue grows over time.

---

## 1. The core idea

TradeVerified sits between two groups who both gain from trust:

- **Tradespeople** want to be seen, verified, and hired at better rates.
- **Employers / customers** want to hire skilled workers without the risk of a bad hire.

We charge the side that gets the most economic value from each transaction. Verification is the product — it lowers risk for employers and raises earning power for tradespeople, and both are willing to pay for that.

---

## 2. Revenue streams

### Stream 1 — Tradesperson subscriptions (Pro) — *primary recurring revenue*

| Plan | Price | What they get |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Basic profile, limited portfolio uploads, can apply to jobs |
| **Pro** | **$19/month** ($190/yr) | Unlimited portfolio uploads, featured placement, verification badge, profile analytics, priority in search |

- Free tier drives volume and SEO. Pro converts the workers who treat their trade as a business.
- **Annual billing** (2 months free) improves cash flow and reduces churn.

### Stream 2 — Employer subscriptions — *highest revenue per account*

| Plan | Price | What they get |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Employer** | **$99/month** ($990/yr) | Unlimited job posts, full candidate search & filters, save candidates, contact workers, view portfolios |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Multiple seats, ATS/HR integrations, bulk hiring, dedicated support, API access |

- Employers have budgets and a clear ROI (one avoided bad hire pays for years of subscription).
- Enterprise is sales-led for franchises, staffing agencies, and large contractors.

### Stream 3 — Featured listings & promotions — *high-margin add-ons*

- **Featured tradesperson** placement in marketplace search (à la carte boost).
- **Promoted job posts** for employers who want a role at the top of the board.
- **Spotlight / homepage placement** for both sides.

### Stream 4 — Verification & certification services

- **Expedited verification** fee for tradespeople who want their badge fast.
- **Background / license check** add-on (partner with a verification provider, take a margin).
- **Certification partnerships** — referral fees from trade schools and cert bodies (e.g., EPA 608, OSHA, NATE).

### Stream 5 — Transactional / marketplace (future)

- **Lead generation**: charge employers or customers per qualified contact/application.
- **Booking / quote requests** from end customers with a take rate.
- **Payments**: optional escrow/invoicing with a small processing margin.

### Stream 6 — Ancillary / partnership revenue (future)

- **Insurance, tools, financing, and supply partners** — affiliate/referral commissions to a high-intent trade audience.
- **Job-board syndication** and data insights (aggregate, anonymized labor-market reports).

---

## 3. Pricing logic

- **Free for tradespeople to join** → maximize supply, which makes the marketplace valuable to employers.
- **Charge employers the most** → they capture the largest dollar value (avoiding a ~$17K bad hire).
- **Pro for tradespeople is a "business expense"** priced below one hour of billed work — easy to justify.
- **Annual discounts** to lock in revenue and cut churn.

---

## 4. Example revenue model (illustrative)

Assume modest year-1 traction:

| Segment | Count | Price/mo | Monthly | Annual |
|---------|-------|----------|---------|--------|
| Pro tradespeople | 500 | $19 | $9,500 | $114,000 |
| Employers | 150 | $99 | $14,850 | $178,200 |
| Featured/promoted add-ons | — | — | ~$3,000 | $36,000 |
| **Total** | | | **~$27,350/mo** | **~$328,200/yr** |

Scale the same model 10×, and the platform clears ~$3M ARR with the *same* product — the marginal cost of each new subscriber is near zero.

**Key levers:** number of Pro/Employer accounts, free→paid conversion rate, and churn.

---

## 5. Metrics that matter

- **MRR / ARR** — recurring revenue, the headline number.
- **Free → Pro conversion rate** — target 3–8% of active tradespeople.
- **Employer activation** — % of signups that post a job within 7 days.
- **Churn** — monthly cancellations (watch seasonal trades).
- **LTV : CAC** — lifetime value vs. acquisition cost; aim for 3:1+.
- **Liquidity** — applications per job, and hires per employer (the marketplace health metric).

---

## 6. Why this works (the moat)

- **Two-sided network effect**: more verified workers → more employers → more workers.
- **Verification is sticky**: badges, reviews, and history accumulate value over time, raising switching costs.
- **Low marginal cost**: software scales; revenue grows far faster than expense.
- **Multiple expansion paths**: subscriptions today, transactions and partnerships tomorrow.

---

## 7. Rollout sequence

1. **Phase 1 (now):** Subscriptions (Pro + Employer) via Stripe. Get to liquidity in a few metros/trades.
2. **Phase 2:** Featured/promoted listings + expedited verification.
3. **Phase 3:** Enterprise plans + certification/insurance partnerships.
4. **Phase 4:** Transactional revenue (leads, bookings, payments) once volume supports it.

> Start narrow (one or two trades in one region), prove the loop, then expand trade-by-trade and city-by-city.
